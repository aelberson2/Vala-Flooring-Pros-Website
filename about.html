Our Story

A better way to choose new flooring

Vala Flooring was created with a simple goal: to make choosing and installing new flooring easier, more personal, and more dependable.

Why This Model Works

We bring experienced design consultants directly into your home

We believe homeowners should have a better way to explore their options without the pressure and confusion that can come with a traditional showroom experience. Instead of asking customers to navigate endless products on their own, we bring experienced design consultants directly into your home.

Our design consultants bring decades of industry experience and take a personalized approach to every project. We begin by learning about your home, your lifestyle, your design preferences, and what you need from your flooring. With that understanding, we help you select flooring that complements your space, fits your style, and holds up to everyday life.

One Point of Accountability

Service that's just as reliable as the flooring itself

We believe the service should be just as reliable as the flooring itself. That is why we stay involved throughout the entire process, from measurements and product selection to installation and the final details. You will not have to coordinate between multiple companies or wonder who is responsible for the next step. We keep communication clear, stay organized, and make sure every part of the project is handled with care.

Our ability to provide that level of service is strengthened through our relationship with Ultimate Home Services. As part of a national home services network, the partnership gives Vala access to extensive industry resources and experience, including more than 100 years of combined senior flooring leadership experience and over 80,000 completed jobs each year.

The result is a flooring experience built around expertise, convenience, and accountability, giving you confidence from your first consultation through the completed installation.
